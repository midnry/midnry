## Things that change a conclusion

- **Wave:** "a Nigerian business cannot use Wave" is too strong. Existing accounts outside the US/CA can still create unlimited invoices and share them as a PDF or link, but cannot send them through Wave and get no payments. New users outside the US/CA are pushed to Zoho Books.
- **Todoist:** its web app is officially **not supported in phone browsers**. Do not list it as a browser alternative for phone-only users.
- **Google AI Plus:** do not quote US$4.99. Google says the price varies by country, and no official naira or USD price for Nigeria was found.
- **Splitwise:** the free tier has a daily expense cap, so it is not "unlimited free".
- **Bumpa:** there is no free plan, only a 14-day trial, and there is no monthly billing. The cheapest entry is ₦15,000 up front per quarter.
- **Medisafe:** a web "Patient Portal" exists, so it is not strictly native-only. Clue, Flo and MyTherapy cannot be used for tracking in a browser.
