# Midnry kit apps vs established alternatives

Research date: all pages read on **23 October 2026** unless a different date is stated. Sources are vendor/official pages only; where a page could not be fetched the comparison is marked **INCOMPLETE**. Nothing here was tested hands-on; "what it does" is what the vendor page says. Naira figures are quoted as published; no exchange-rate conversion is asserted.

Midnry baseline (from the brief): free with an account; "Midnry Pass" US$5/month; browser apps aimed at phone users in Nigeria/West Africa.

## B. Nigerian tax and finance calculators

### Official 2026 PAYE figures (for later checking of Midnry's Tax kit — correctness not asserted here)

Source: **Nigeria Tax Act, 2025 (Act No. 7), Official Gazette No. 117, Vol. 112, 26 June 2025**, gazetted PDF hosted by the National Assembly [B1] (downloaded and text-extracted 23 Oct 2026). Commencement printed on the Act: **"[1st January, 2026]"** [B1].

**Fourth Schedule (Section 58) — Individuals' income tax rates**, quoted:
> "After the relief allowance and exemptions had been granted in accordance with sub-section of (1) of section 30 of this Act, the taxable income ascertained shall be taxed at the following rates –
> (a) First N800,000 at 0% ; (b) Next N2,200,000 at 15% ; (c) Next N9,000,000 at 18% ; (d) Next N13,000,000 at 21% ; (e) Next N25,000,000 at 23% ; and (f) Above N50,000,000 at 25%." [B1]

**Section 58**: "The income tax payable on the chargeable income of an individual, other than an individual earning the Minimum Wage in line with the Minimum Wage Act, in respect of each year of assessment, shall be as specified in the Fourth Schedule to this Act." [B1]

**Section 30(2)(a) — eligible deductions**, quoted: contributions under the National Housing Fund; National Health Insurance Scheme; Pension Reform Act; "interest on loans for developing an owner-occupied residential house"; life-assurance/deferred-annuity premiums; and "(vi) rent relief of 20% of annual rent paid, subject to a maximum of N500,000, whichever is lower, provided that the individual accurately declares the actual amount of rent paid and other relevant information as may be prescribed by the relevant tax authority" [B1].

**Section 163(1)(t) — exempt income**: "income of a person from an employment where such person earns gross income of national minimum wage or less from such employment" [B1].

**Section 147 — VAT**: "VAT shall be charged on the value of all taxable supplies at the rate of 7.5%." [B1]

Corroborating professional summaries:
- **KPMG GMS Flash Alert 2025-168 (15 Sep 2025)**: same six bands expressed as ranges (0–₦800,000 0%; ₦800,001–₦3,000,000 15%; ₦3,000,001–₦12,000,000 18%; ₦12,000,001–₦25,000,000 21%; ₦25,000,001–₦50,000,000 23%; over ₦50,000,000 25%); "The NTA eliminates the Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA) and introduces the rent relief of 20 percent of annual rent paid, capped at ₦500,000"; "Homeowners and others who do not pay rent are not eligible"; effective "from January 2026" [B2].
- **PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries – Nigeria, Individual** (last reviewed 29 May 2026): same table; "The removal of the consolidated relief allowance and the introduction of the rent relief"; old 1% minimum tax "has effectively been replaced by the expanded 0% tax band"; employees earning "not more than the national minimum wage (NGN 70,000)" are "no longer liable to tax or deduction of monthly PAYE" [B3][B4]. (The ₦70,000 figure is PwC's statement; the Minimum Wage Act itself was not read.)
- **PwC – Nigeria, Other taxes**: "The standard VAT rate is 7.5% (increased from 5% on 1 February 2020)" [B5].
- **Forvis Mazars Nigeria (21 Apr 2026)**: rent relief "20% of annual rent paid, capped at ₦500,000", s. 30(2)(a)(vi); available to tenants only; cites Joint Revenue Board guidelines issued 7 April 2026 [B6].
- **Mondaq / Adeola Oyinlade & Co (5 Jan 2026)**: worked example — ₦60,000,000 chargeable income gives total tax ₦12,930,000 [B7] (useful as a test vector for Midnry's Tax kit).

**INCOMPLETE**: the Joint Revenue Board "Personal Income Tax Guidelines 2026" PDF (link given by Orbitax, [B8]) returned 404 on 23 Oct 2026, and the NRS "Individual Income Tax" page contained navigation only [B9]; official PAYE administrative guidance therefore could not be quoted.
