# Outpost Zero tactical 3D preview assets

This is a browser WebGL game. Actual rendering resolution and frame rate depend on hardware and display refresh. The operator now uses original contemporary uniform/equipment geometry with a licensed scanned head and separate physical materials; the earlier Vanguard armor geometry and painted body atlas are not rendered.

## Physically based photographs and sky

Poly Haven publishes these assets under CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license.
The bundled maps use the 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness photographs:

- Ground: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_asphalt_01
- Corrugated wall panels: https://polyhaven.com/a/corrugated_iron_03
- Weapon metal: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate
- Gloves: https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_01
- HDR sky / image-based lighting: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy

## Character and animation

The realism overhaul adds substrate-specific maps listed in [REALISM-ASSETS.md](REALISM-ASSETS.md).

`models/soldier.glb` is the Mixamo Soldier/Vanguard model distributed in the Three.js skinning/blending example:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb
https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_animation_skinning_blending.html

The example credits Mixamo. Character assets are subject to Adobe Mixamo terms, separate from Three.js's code license. Use this model as an embedded game character, not as a downloadable asset product. Source and terms:
https://www.mixamo.com/
https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html

`models/operator-v1.glb` is generated by `scripts/build-outpost-operator.mjs`. Its uniform, carrier, helmet, gloves, footwear and equipment are original geometry. It retains the embedded Mixamo skeleton and idle/walk/run clips from the source above; those animation resources remain subject to Mixamo terms.

The head is **Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith**, based on work at **www.triplegangers.com**, licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**. Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith . Original permission text: [HEAD-SCAN-LICENSE.txt](HEAD-SCAN-LICENSE.txt). Modifications: the shoulder bust is cropped at the neck; head geometry is rescaled, reoriented and bound to the operator rig. The original colour and tangent normal maps are used; an appropriately restrained roughness map is derived from the provided specular photograph for PBR skin. The scan source is retained as `models/head-source.glb` for reproducible builds. CC BY 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . The face scan is not an endorsement by its creator.
